Makubaliano Ya Siri

By Layla Pendo · 5 chapters · Free to read

Also available: English

Zawadi Mrema na Hassan Mollel walijenga Mavuno Link kwa miaka minane kwa mikopo, farmer trust na malori ya kukodi. Wakati Zawadi anapeleka malori matatu ya mahindi kwa Kiwanda cha Unga Kaskazini huku malipo ya zamani bado hayajalipwa, factory manager anasema account ilifungwa tayari. Settlement ina saini halisi ya Hassan. Zawadi anafikiri partner wake ameiba au kuuza kampuni kwa nusu bei, lakini ledger inaonyesha baadhi ya delivery differences ni za kweli. Hassan anapopatikana, anaficha kwamba settlement money ilienda kuzuia benki kuchukua nyumba yake—na nyumba hiyo ndiyo iliyowahi kuwekwa collateral wakati kampuni ilikuwa karibu kufa miaka iliyopita. Zawadi lazima atenganishe shukrani, betrayal, verified debt na future authority bila kuharibu farmers waliomwamini.
