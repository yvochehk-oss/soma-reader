Salim Mwinyi can repair a phone that has fallen into seawater, but he cannot repair the month in which three customers refuse to pay, his kiosk rent rises, and a box of replacement screens arrives defective. When an old acquaintance tells him about Bwana Tunu—someone who supposedly helps struggling people clear the blockage around business—Salim laughs first. Then he pays once...
