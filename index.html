Free bilingual web novels (English & Kiswahili), re-typeset English classics, and a free self-publishing uploader. / Riwaya za bure za mtandaoni (Kiingereza na Kiswahili), vitabu vya kale vilivyopangwa upya, na kifungu cha bure cha kuchapisha mwenyewe.

Soma Novel is a free reading site from East Africa. It does three things, and they are the only things it does: 1) bilingual web fiction (爽文) published side-by-side in English and Kiswahili, fully free to read and free to download; 2) more than a thousand public-domain English classics professionally re-typeset for phone screens, free to read and free to download (English only — the classics section does not produce Kiswahili translations because the source language is English); 3) a self-publishing uploader that accepts .txt and .epub in either English or Kiswahili, no account required.

Browse the full catalogue of free novels, or jump to a popular category: Romance, Thriller, Fantasy, Sci-Fi, Historical, Contemporary.

Every book page includes a synopsis, chapter list, author attribution, and a direct link to the first chapter. Readers can save books to a personal shelf, adjust the reader’s font size, theme, and line spacing, and download the entire book (TXT or EPUB) for offline reading.

What you can do here / Unachoweza kufanya hapa

Read free. Every original novel is published in English and Kiswahili side-by-side. / Soma bure. Kila riwaya ya asili inachapishwa kwa Kiingereza na Kiswahili kwa pamoja.

Download free. Download any book as a complete TXT or EPUB file, with no account required. / Pakua bure. Pakua kitabu chochote kama faili kamili la TXT au EPUB, bila akaunti.

Read classics on your phone. Over a thousand public-domain English works, re-typeset for phone screens with cleaner line spacing and a distraction-free reader. (English only — Kiswahili translations are not produced for the classics section.) / Soma vitabu vya kawaida kwenye simu yako. Publish your own. Upload a .txt or .epub file through the in-page uploader and start reading it immediately. / Chapisha yako mwenyewe. Pakia faili la .txt au .epub kupitia kifungu cha kupakia na uanze kusoma mara moja.
